Metasurfaces · RF → THz → Optics

Unlocking the potential of electromagnetic phenomena, from RF to optics.

We manipulate electromagnetic waves at will using metasurfaces — ultrathin artificial electromagnetic surfaces that sculpt fields with sub-wavelength precision — for next-generation 6G communications, satellite links, terahertz systems, and high-resolution imaging.

Research

Wave control as a design problem

Our work spans microwave, millimeter-wave, terahertz, and optical regimes — treating mutual coupling and nonlocality not as nuisances, but as engineering resources.

Wave physics

Nonlocal and spatially-dispersive metasurfaces

Most metasurfaces are designed cell by cell, treating coupling between neighboring elements as a nuisance. We take the opposite view. By deliberately engineering nonlocality — the collective response of many coupled cells — we access functionalities that local designs cannot reach, including angle-selective responses and extreme wave transformations.

6G · Smart environments

Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces

Reconfigurable intelligent surfaces turn ordinary walls and facades into programmable radio mirrors that route wireless signals on demand. We study how such surfaces can shape entire communication channels — across multiple frequency bands and polarizations — to extend coverage and capacity in next-generation networks.

Antennas · Microwave

Metasurface antennas

Metasurfaces enable antennas that are thin, light, and free of the bulky feed networks behind conventional phased arrays. We develop aperture architectures that radiate directly from guided and cavity modes, aiming at low-cost, power-efficient front ends for communications and sensing.

THz

Terahertz wave engineering

The terahertz band promises ultra-broadband links and fine-grained sensing, but generating and steering THz waves efficiently remains a challenge. We combine metasurfaces, leaky-wave antennas, and nonlinear optics to generate, radiate, and steer terahertz beams within a single integrated platform.

Computational

Computational and AI-assisted design

As metasurface apertures grow in scale and complexity, brute-force simulation becomes the bottleneck. We build physics-aware modeling and inverse-design frameworks — from integral-equation methods to deep learning — that make large, strongly coupled apertures tractable.
